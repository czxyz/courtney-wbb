          How players are picked

          Each team's top three players by points, rebounds and assists. Players with 10 or more games last season are rated on last season, blended a little with this season. Everyone else is rated on this season so far, so early numbers can swing. Transfers show the school their numbers came from.

          Apple, Google and Outlook calendars

          Calendar

          Games
          Remind

          You get a .zip with one calendar file inside. On iPhone, open it in Files, tap courtney-schedule.ics, then Add All. On a computer, unzip it and open the file, or import it in Google Calendar under Settings › Import. To add one game to Google Calendar, tap it on the schedule.

          Game times can move, especially for TV. Save a fresh file if your teams' times change.
